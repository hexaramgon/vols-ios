Volspire is where independent artists make music together — from a voice memo in your bedroom to a finished release.

Upload your tracks, share stems with collaborators, find artists near you, and get real feedback right on the music. No label, no gatekeepers. Just you, your sound, and the people you want to make it with.

MAKE MUSIC TOGETHER
• Send a collab request to any artist and start a conversation that's built around the music
• Post on the collab board when you need a vocalist, a mix, a topline or a beat to write over — and answer other artists' calls
• Find producers, rappers, singers and engineers near you on the collab map
• Keep track of everyone you've worked with in one place

YOUR WORKSPACE
• Shared folders for stems, bounces, demos and references — invite your collaborators as editors or viewers
• Comment on any file, and see who uploaded, commented or joined across your folders
• Share a folder with anyone using a public link — no account needed. Choose whether downloads are allowed and when the link expires, and turn it off any time

UPLOAD IN SECONDS
• Post audio or video straight from your camera roll or Files
• Videos are compressed on your phone, with full-quality sound
• Trim a video before posting, or upload just its audio
• Credit everyone who worked on the track
• Choose who hears it: public, unlisted or private
• Group tracks into releases — EPs, albums, beat tapes — and show them on your profile

FEEDBACK WHERE IT MATTERS
• Timed comments pin feedback to the exact moment in a track
• Comments show up as dots on the progress bar and pop up as the track plays
• Artist Dashboard: plays, listeners, average listen time and how people found each track

A PLAYER BUILT FOR ARTISTS
• Full-screen video, swipe to the next track
• Play next, add to queue, and drag to reorder
• Speed and pitch controls, plus a 5-band EQ
• Shuffle, repeat and lock-screen controls

FREE SAMPLE PACKS
• Share your samples, loops and presets as packs — pick your files and they're zipped for you
• Download packs from other artists and drop them straight into a Workspace folder
• Leave reviews, and see views, downloads and saves on your own packs

MADE FOR THE UNDERGROUND
Volspire is for bedroom producers, beatmakers, rappers, singers, DJs and everyone making music on their own terms. Pick your roles, build your profile, and let people find you for what you actually do.

• Tracks, Short Tracks and Snippets shelves, so every idea has a home — finished songs, short loops and sketches
• Follow artists, and get notified about new followers, comments, messages and collab requests
• Light and dark mode

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Report or block anyone, from any track, comment, profile or message
• You control your visibility on the collab map — your exact location is never shared
• Delete your account any time, right in the app

Volspire is free to use. Upload your first track today and start making something with someone.
