# Volspire 1.1.0 — What's New

App Store Connect "What's New in This Version" copy for iOS 1.1.0 (build 11).
Covers everything since the 1.0 launch commit (df6f2e9): e533433 "updated ui"
and 34d0dd3 "more changes". Flag-gated features (paid marketplace, visualizer,
profile banners) are intentionally left out.

Check before pasting: the artist map is live on iOS, and pack/listing
analytics are visible with the marketplace flag off. Cut those lines if not.

## Full version

Volspire 1.1 — a big one.

**A fresh new look**
• Redesigned app, top to bottom, with a cleaner layout and new typography
• Light mode is here — follow your system or pick one in Settings

**Now Playing**
• Queue: see what's up next, drag to reorder, remove tracks, or clear it
• "Play next" and "Add to queue" from any track's menu
• 5-band EQ alongside speed and pitch in Audio Effects
• Timed comments now show on the progress bar and pop up as the track plays
• Track credits and an About card for every song
• Full-screen mode for video tracks
• Haptic feedback on shuffle and repeat

**Releases**
• Group your tracks into releases: create, reorder, and show them on your profile

**Explore**
• New Home with the collab board, artist map, and search all in one place
• Network page for your followers and collaborators

**Sample packs**
• Download free sample packs and leave reviews
• Pack and listing analytics for creators

**Workspace**
• Redesigned folders with an activity feed
• Share folders with a public link

**Uploads**
• Pick videos straight from your camera roll, with automatic compression
• Add tracks to playlists faster

**Plus** faster loading, better offline support, and lots of fixes.

## Short version

A redesigned Volspire with light mode, a play queue with drag-to-reorder and
"Play next," a 5-band EQ, timed comments on the progress bar, releases, free
sample packs, folder share links, and lots of speed improvements and fixes.
